How we work together

  1. You send me the material. Text, video or audio, in whatever format you have. I tell you what I would do with it, how long it takes and what it costs.
  2. I research before I write. I build a glossary for your project, check how the terms are used in your market and ask you anything that is ambiguous.
  3. You get it ready to publish. Delivered in the format you need, with my notes on the choices that matter, and time for one round of changes.
