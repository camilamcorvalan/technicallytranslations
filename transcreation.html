Transcreation

Marketing and promotional content adapted, not just translated, so it keeps its voice, its humor and its impact in the other language.

What I can help with

  • Ad copy & slogans
  • Social media campaigns
  • Sponsorship materials
  • Product launches
  • Team announcements

How I work

  1. Understand the goal. What the message should make the audience feel or do.
  2. Recreate. I look for wordplay, tone and references that work in the target culture.
  3. Explain. You get my choices explained, so you know why each line works.

Sample work

#Frankly, our new credit card gives users the chance to have a truly free alternative, with no hidden issuing or maintenance costs. This limited edition has the signature of one of the greatest, Franco Colapinto.
