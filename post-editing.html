Service 03

Machine translation post-editing

Machine translation is fast; post-editing makes it right. I review machine output so it reads naturally, uses the correct terminology and says exactly what the source says.

What I can help with

  • Full post-editing for publication
  • Light post-editing for internal use
  • Terminology correction
  • Fluency & register adjustments
  • Consistency with your glossaries

How I work

  1. Agree on the level. Light or full post-editing, depending on how the text will be used.
  2. Edit. I correct meaning, terminology and fluency against the source.
  3. Deliver. A text that reads as if it had been written in Spanish.
