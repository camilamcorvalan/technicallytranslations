See it in practice

Mercado Pago × Franco Colapinto. A personal project: my English transcreation of Mercado Pago Argentina's #TeSoyFranco campaign. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Mercado Pago.

Before · Spanish

#TeSoyFranco con nuestra tarjeta de crédito los usuarios tienen acceso a una alternativa gratuita de verdad, sin costos de emisión ni mantenimiento. Y en esta edición limitada, con la firma de un grande como Franco Colapinto.

After · English

#Frankly, our new credit card gives users the chance to have a truly free alternative, with no hidden issuing or maintenance costs. This limited edition has the signature of one of the greatest, Franco Colapinto.

The campaign hinged on a pun: "te soy franco" means "I'm being frank with you" and also names the driver. "#Frankly" keeps both readings in English.
